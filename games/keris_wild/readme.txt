Keris Wilds: Pusaka Sang Raja — v2.2 RGS MathLock

5x4, 40 fixed lines. Keris Wild expands on reels 2-4 and awards one base
respin. Free spins use per-reel sticky wilds whose multipliers grow from 1x to
3x; paid-line multipliers are products capped at 20x.

Modes:
- base: 1x, BR0, locked RTP 0.967118
- ante: 1.5x, BR1, locked RTP 0.959566
- bonus_buy: 72x, direct 10 free spins, locked RTP 0.967703

The v2.2 paytable is retuned to exact 0.10x increments for the RGS payout
contract. Reel strips and all feature mechanics remain byte-for-byte unchanged.

The long-form reel CSV is the exact published asset and is SHA-256 verified at
config load. `run.py` intentionally defaults to a 100-book, one-worker,
uncompressed smoke run with optimization and analysis disabled.
